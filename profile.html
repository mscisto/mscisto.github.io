About Me

I'm Mateo Sciscento, an Economics and Spanish graduate from the University of Nevada, Reno, with a background in esports production and a growing passion for computer science and game development. My goal is to become a gameplay systems engineer, combining my experience in competitive gaming with technical problem-solving and programming.

Outside of work and coding, I'm an avid Real Madrid and tennis fan. You'll usually find me running, playing tennis, or yelling at Madrid during a match.
